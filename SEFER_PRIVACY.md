# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 13, 2026
**App:** Sefer
**Developer:** Joshua Grzybowski

## Overview

Sefer is a native Windows desktop reader for Markdown documents. Your privacy is important — Sefer does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data or usage analytics.

## Data Storage

All data created by Sefer is stored **locally on your device** and never sent to external servers. This includes:

- **Your library** — books and documents you add to Sefer remain on your device
- **Reading progress and "Lately read" history** — stored locally
- **Bookmarks, highlights, and annotations** — stored locally
- **Application logs** — stored locally for troubleshooting purposes
- **User preferences** — theme, typography, layout, and configuration settings stored locally

You can delete this data at any time by removing items within the app or by deleting Sefer's local data directory under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Sefer`.

## Data Collection

Sefer does **not** collect:

- Personal information
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports sent to external services
- Advertising or tracking data
- Cookies or browser fingerprints
- Information about which documents you read or how long you read them

## Network Communication

Sefer makes **no network requests** in normal use. The bundled public-domain library that ships with Sefer is included with the app itself — no download occurs at runtime.

If a future version of Sefer adds optional online features (such as fetching a remote document by URL), those features will be initiated explicitly by you, and this policy will be updated to describe them.

## Third-Party Content

Sefer ships with a small bundled library of public-domain works. These texts are provided as-is for your convenience and are not associated with any third-party service.

Documents you open from your own filesystem are read locally; Sefer does not transmit their contents anywhere.

## Children's Privacy

Sefer does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

Updates to this privacy policy will be reflected in this document with an updated effective date. Continued use of Sefer after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
